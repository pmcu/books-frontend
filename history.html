History of Keady

Over a period of more that 100 years, number of books, booklets and other written material have made interesting contributions to documumenting and commenting on Keady's local history. Much or most of this material is no longer available. It would be a good idea to change this situation.

For many people interested, Henry Dobbin's Keady 100 years ago will be the first published book that comes to mind. Dobbin's wee book was first published by the Royal Irish Academy in the early 1920s. At the bottom of his preface he puts the words 'H. J. Dobbin, Rathcarbry House, Christmas 1921'. These words themselves are now a piece of early 20th century history.
