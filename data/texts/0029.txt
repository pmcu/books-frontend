At the Vestry, held in the Parish Church on Easter
Monday in 1818, the Rector (1) (Rev. E. $. Roberts),
presiding, John Rule and Jas. Kidd were elected church-
wardens, the outgoing wardens being James Dobbin,
jun., and Samuel Sturgeon. The Vestry clerk was George
Cuming. His salary was £2 5s. 6d., while the church-
wardens received one pound each. The Vestry thus
anticipating Parliament in its payment of members by
a hundred years.
