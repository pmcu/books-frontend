I think our forefathers with all their lack of present
day conveniences were on the whole happy and contented,
and in some ways at least to be envied. As a man put
it to me, perhaps a little abruptly :— "Why, man, you're
talkin" about old times, but them was the times, none
like them since. Beef at tuppence (1) a pound and
whisky for nothin' — fippence a naggin — and poteen galore,
and what the hell more could any man want?" Here
is a conception of happiness which [ am afraid a great
many will hardly agree with.
