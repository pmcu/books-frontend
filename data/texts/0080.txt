The following list (typical of the changes in Keady
during the past 100 years) gives some of the Old Families
resident in the district at the time. As a means of
identification the name of the present residents are also
given. In some cases the old houses have disappeared,
the present houses being built on or near to the old sites.
At that date there were 125 houses in the town, and, a
Population of 583 (279 being males and 304 females).
