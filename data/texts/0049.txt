Previous to the building of the "Old Chapel" Mass,
was often celebrated beside a well, still known as the
"Altar Well," a short distance from where the old Gas
Works stood; a rocky ledge served as an altar. When
I was a boy an old man showed me where the priest stood,
and told me that when he was a child his father brought
him there one day and pointed out the spot where, a
generation before, he himself, as a boy, had served Mass.
