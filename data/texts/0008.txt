Then, as now, Linen Manufacture (2) was the staple
industry of Keady ; but the operations of spinning and
weaving were all carried out in the homes of the workers.
Almost every house had its loom, sometimes 3 or 4, or
more. As the price of a handloom was only about 30/-
the cost of the plant necessary to equip a workshop was not
very formidable. Coote, remarks how unhealthy the
trade had become through the effect of the Window Tax :—
"It is to be regretted that the weaver is obliged to shut
up so many windows and chimneys of his house to evade
the taxes, as in many of these cottages there is no
admittance of air but through the door."
