A Parliamentary election nowadays would appear a very
tame affair to our great-grandfathers, who had to go to
Armagh to record their votes, and considered themselves
lucky if they arrived back in Keady with a whole skin,
as the votes had to be recorded in open court, and large
bodies of men armed with sticks held the front of the
building. Those voting for a candidate disapproved of by
the strongest faction undertook a risk.
