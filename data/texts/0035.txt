I may seem strange that the Vestry should insert this
in a Newry paper ; but there was no newspaper published
in Armagh at this time, and the Newry Telegraph (then
under the editorship of James Stuart, the historian of Armagh)
was the only paper circulating in the district. Wolfe's
famous poem, "The Burial of Sir John Moore," made
its first appearance in print in this journal in 1817.
